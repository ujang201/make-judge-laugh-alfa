Write one original joke about office life, a single line under 200 characters, to be read aloud in one breath.

It must be:
- squarely about office life (meetings, email, bosses, deadlines) so the listener nods before laughing;
- built on a true, recognizable observation, not a generic pun;
- surprising in its last words — a fresh turn, not a stock line with the nouns swapped;
- tight: no wasted setup, every word earning its place.

Best angle: the meeting that could have been an email. Twist it so that complaining about the meeting only creates more meetings and more emails — a loop the office can never escape.
